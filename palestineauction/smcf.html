SMCF

Sean Middlebrough Charity Foundation

SMCF — the Sean Middlebrough Charity Foundation — is the humble beginning behind this listings board. Supporters sell their stuff; the proceeds help activists who are in prison, and their families.

Everything here operates within the rules set out on the legality & disclaimer page: only legally owned goods, legally sold, and nothing on this board is for or connected to any proscribed organisation.
