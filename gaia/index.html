Why now

A great deal of new money, all at once

Anthropic has filed to go public. A listing of that size turns years of paper equity into spendable wealth for a large number of people in a short space of time.

There is more money than purposeful things you can buy.Our belief

Land is one of the few purchases whose purpose does not wear out. It holds water, grows food and gives shelter. We propose converting part of this new wealth into land that stays in common ownership.

How it works

Shares in, land out

All of the money goes towards land.

  1. PledgeState publicly what you intend to give. Non-binding.
  2. TransferDonate shares or proceeds once they are liquid and a receiving entity is named.
  3. PurchaseEach land purchase is announced publicly before it happens.
  4. CommonsThe land is held by Gaia and is not resold.

Governance

Optimistic trust

Every action is announced in public before it is taken. If nobody objects within 24 hours, that is treated as consent to proceed. An objection pauses the action until it is resolved in the open.

Unresolved objections go to the Independent Tribunal of the Planetary Council.
