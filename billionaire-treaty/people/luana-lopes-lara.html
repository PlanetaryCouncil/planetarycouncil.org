Luana Lopes Lara is a Brazilian citizen who lives in New York. After high school, she spent nine months as a professional ballerina at the Salzburger Landestheater in Austria, performing in a season of "Swan Lake." She is an MIT graduate and a former quantitative trader at Citadel Securities.

She is a cofounder and the chief operating officer of Kalshi, a prediction-market platform. Forbes lists her as the world's youngest self-made woman billionaire. Kalshi lets users bet on outcomes ranging from sports results to elections to entertainment awards. Forbes estimates that she owns about 12 percent of the company.

Kalshi received federal approval to operate in the United States in 2020. In 2024, it received federal approval to offer contracts on the presidential election, the first legal election contracts in the US in more than a century. In December 2025, the company reached an $11 billion valuation after a $1 billion funding round. Its investors include angel backers Charles Schwab and Henry Kravis, along with the firms Andreessen Horowitz, Sequoia Capital, CapitalG and Paradigm.
