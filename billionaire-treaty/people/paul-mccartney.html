Sir Paul McCartney is a former member of The Beatles. He was knighted in 1997. In 2024, the Sunday Times Rich List said he was the first UK musician to reach a fortune of one billion pounds.

The Sunday Times put Paul McCartney and his wife, Nancy Shevell, at £1 billion in 2024, up from £950 million in 2023, when they ranked 175th on the list. The newspaper credited the rise to strong touring, a valuable back catalogue and a little help from Beyoncé, who covered the Beatles song Blackbird. The Sunday Times gave £50 million of the couple's total to Nancy Shevell.

In 2009, McCartney and his daughters Stella and Mary launched Meat Free Monday, a campaign to promote meat-free meals at least one day a week.

Under the treaty

The Sunday Times counts Paul McCartney as a billionaire in pounds, at £1 billion for 2024. He does not appear on the Forbes dollar list this register uses, so whether he is above the one-billion-dollar cap depends on the list and the exchange rate.
