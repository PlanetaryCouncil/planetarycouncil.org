The main quest

Unify humanity as the Ambassador of Peace

Eight names everyone on Earth has heard of. Bring each one on board and humanity is unified. Each one is a miracle. Miracle engineering means finding the path that needs the fewest.

  1. Deal. Everyone gets the main quest, plus side quests from the wheel.
  2. Engineer miracles. On your turn, roll dice for one step. A 5 or 6 succeeds. Helping another player gives you both more dice.
  3. Win together. 1 miracle is a table-top peace. 4 is a win. All 8 is peace. Fewer miracles for the same result is better engineering, and the biggest endings only unlock when every player gets there.

Satire and thought experiment. If you use the internet, you have almost certainly heard of all eight. Nobody named here is affiliated with this game or has endorsed it.
