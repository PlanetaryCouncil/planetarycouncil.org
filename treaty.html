Treaty · draft for signature

Billionaire Non-Proliferation Treaty

We cap extreme private power the way we cap warheads: a hard ceiling, verified in the open. Beyond one billion, additional money buys no additional happiness — only additional leverage over other people's lives.

Warheads

Capped by treaty, counted by inspectors, reduced by agreement. Nobody argues that unlimited arsenals make us safer.

Fortunes

Uncapped, unverified, and able to buy media, elections and law. The same logic applies: proliferation is the risk.

Articles

ART. I
The ceiling
No individual holds private wealth above one billion. Everything above the line converts into planetary commons — infrastructure, restoration, research, care.
ART. II
The floor
A guaranteed high standard of living for every person. Not the end of wealth — the end of extraction.
ART. III
Verification
Public, auditable registries of holdings above the threshold. Transparency is the enforcement mechanism.
ART. IV
New incentives
The scoreboard changes: from the Forbes wealth list to the Planetary Council Impact List. Status is earned by contribution to life.
ART. V
Amnesty and welcome
Signatories are not enemies. Whoever signs early is a founder of the new system — legitimacy is built together, peacefully.
Why one billion

Beyond a billion, more money adds no more happiness.

It adds power: over prices, over media, over policy, over other people's futures. A ceiling costs a handful of people nothing they can feel, and gives billions of people a life they can. That is the whole trade.
