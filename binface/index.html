ABOUT — HOW THIS ARTWORK THINKS

The four commandments

The square is sacred — full brightness, nothing covers it. The data is real — every reaction comes from pre-baked stem analysis and pre-baked pixel analysis, no fake randomness. Every version is kept — this page is one of ~50 numbered iterations, each preserved byte-for-byte. Everything is deterministic in song time — seek anywhere, the artwork is identical; captures reproduce exactly.

The journey

It began as a waveform toy, went through a ChatGPT reference build, then a Claude rebuild that discovered the CHRONO PORTAL — a feedback dimension where red is the recent past, green the present, blue the deep past. The ULTIMA line (01–21) added a scene director that reads the real cuts of the edit and casts a field language per shot — MIRROR, PORTAL, DROSTE's infinite corridor of squares — then octopus gravity, star-boom lyrics, and an achievements engine. The PURE line stripped everything back: the video at full height, still and sharp, lyrics whose letters hold still while their RGB shadows dance, achievements that simply happen — authored inside the lyrics file itself.

The two ears

The artwork listens twice. Ten stems were analysed offline into 7,719 frames of energy, onsets and bands — VOICE, CHOIR, RHYTHM, BASS, MUSIC each drive their own limb. Then the PICTURE was analysed too: every frame split into a 10×10 grid and scored for busyness, differentiation, palette, colourfulness, contrast and motion. At every audio peak the METRIC ROULETTE turns to the next picture metric, and that metric drives the prism — the busier the frame, the deeper the mirror mosaic cuts. Audio decides WHEN. The picture decides HOW MUCH.
